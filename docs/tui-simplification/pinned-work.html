Pinned work without the box

After using P-C's live box, it feels out of step with the rest of the TUI. The direction from that review: the agent's output goes back into the transcript and settles where it was drawn. Only the todos, the plan run, running subagent and job cards, and progress widgets stay pinned at the bottom. Below are three ways to draw that, next to today's box.

  • It looks like a panelThe only bordered, sectioned thing in the conversation, yet it never takes focus like a panel.
  • The step in progress movesIt streams inside the box, then reappears above in another form when it settles.
  • It has its own controlsClick-to-open cards, Ctrl+T's three layouts, and browse running on into the box.
  • Its height jumpsIt grows and shrinks as work starts and stops, pushing history up and down.

Fixed in every option

  • Agent outputStreams at the end of the transcript and settles in place. Narration, thinking and calls never move.
  • FrameNo border and no section headers. Ctrl+T and [tui.live_box] layout go.
  • One plan grammarThe history's plan line, ✓ ✓ ▸ · 3/4, where 3/4 is the current item's position. Today the box's ▰▰▱▱ 2/4 counts finished items instead.
  • Running rowsToday's row format: glyph and id in the kind's colour, title, measurement and clock, on the surface tint.
  • ControlsA pinned row behaves like a transcript row: browse reaches it after the last history row, Enter or a click opens it, s stops it, f opens a subagent.
  • Turn clock and plan runThe clock and stop hint move to the status line. An SDD or swarm run becomes a pinned row like a job: ▸ run task 2/5 · implement 3m.

Decision P-E

The tray (option A), because it keeps the whole todo list in view. No rule above it.

The tray

  • Running rows, then the todo list, directly above the input. The tint on running rows and the plan's marks set the tray apart; there is no rule, which saves a row.
  • The agent's output stays in the transcript. The step in progress streams at the bottom of history and settles where it was drawn.
  • The turn clock and stop hint move to the status line.

Finished items

  • Keep their row until your next prompt. No tint, the outcome in place of the measurement, the clock stopped, and ✓ or ✗ where the spinner was.
  • The completion event still lands in history the moment the item finishes.

Beat heads

  • The meta stays on the narration's first line. A narration too long to sit beside its tag wraps narrower, so the tag never takes a line of its own. Compare “Starting the weights download…” here with the frames below.

The three options

ATray

Today's box without the frame or the live step: a plain rule, the running rows, then the plan list in two columns.

Pinned
Running rows and the full todo list.
Height
Grows when work starts. A finished item keeps its slot, dimmed with its outcome, until the next prompt, so the tray shrinks only between turns.
Controls
Rows only. Enter on a todo jumps to the beat that started it.
Cost
The most rows, and the list is a second plan grammar beside the plan line.

BOne line

Everything pinned shares one line above the input. Cards open in /work.

Pinned
The plan line, then one short segment per running item: a mini bar for measured work, the latest activity for a subagent.
Height
One row whenever a plan or running work exists. It never changes.
Controls
None in the line. A click on it, or /work, opens the Work panel.
Cost
Widgets shrink to a mini bar; three or more items fall back to counts.

CRows on the input Recommended

Running rows sit directly on the input, only while something runs. The plan line moves into the status line.

Pinned
Running rows that open to full cards, and the plan line, right-aligned in the status line.
Height
Nothing extra when nothing runs; one row per running item, with A's keep-the-slot rule.
Controls
Rows only.
Cost
Only the current todo is named. At 80 columns the status line drops the model name.

What today's render shows

  • Three plan drawings. The history's plan line, the box's two-column list and the idle row ▸ Plan 2/4. The box and the plan line give different counts.
  • Room. The box takes 11 of 34 rows when busy. At 80×24 it takes 8 of 24 and pushes your message off the screen.
  • An empty border. Idle with a job still running, the box keeps a bare top border above its running 1 header.

Recommendation

C, with A's keep-the-slot rule (you chose A, for the todo list)

It is the only option that adds nothing when nothing runs. The plan gets one fixed place, and running work keeps full cards with bars, which is what the P-D progress skill draws. If having the whole todo list on screen matters more than the rows it costs, A is the fallback.

What it would take

  • Draw the live part in the transcript again. SplitLive stays in the projection, but the TUI renders history and the live part together, as before P-C.
  • Cut livebox down to the pinned rows. The frame, section headers, layouts and their shed order go, and [tui.live_box] is deprecated with a warning.
  • Keep P-C's running-row and card renderers, browse targets and stop command.
  • Move the turn clock and stop hint to the status line. C moves the plan line there too.
  • P-D needs no change for A or C. Under B, check that /work's card shows the detail and checklist the skill describes.
